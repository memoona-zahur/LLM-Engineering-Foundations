=== PART #9 — Chain-of-Thought vs R1 Unprompted Reasoning: Contrast ===

REFERENCE CORRECT ANSWER:
- Offer 1: 6% flat × $10,000 × 2 years = $1,200 interest → total $11,200
- Offer 2: 4.5% flat × $10,000 × 3 years = $1,350 interest → total $11,350
- LOWER = Offer 1 (by $150)

WHAT LLAMA3.2:3b DID:
- NO-CoT: Said "Offer 2" — WRONG answer, no reasoning shown
- WITH-CoT: Showed steps but calculated interest as 10000 × 0.06 × 24 = $14,400
  (confused months with years in formula). Still wrong but showed reasoning

WHAT DEEPSEEK-R1:8b DID (NO-CoT, thinking enabled):
- Unprompted reasoning: began reasoning correctly (simple interest = $1,200 for Offer 1)
- Then spiraled into an analysis loop about the AMBIGUITY of "flat rate" terminology
  and missing repayment structure for Offer 2
- Never produced a final answer (timed out at 10+ minutes of reasoning)
- The model was not confused by the math but by the REAL ambiguity it detected

TWO-SENTENCE CONTRAST:
"A model that reasons because you asked it to (Llama CoT) follows the requested
step structure mechanically, which can produce wrong math if the formula is wrong but
at least reaches an answer. A model that reasons because that's how it was trained
to respond (R1) will go deeper — in this case detecting genuine ambiguity that a
simpler model misses — but can get stuck in over-analysis rather than reaching
a conclusion. For straightforward tasks, asked-reasoning reaches an answer; for
ambiguous tasks, unprompted reasoning reveals hidden complexity but may never resolve it."
