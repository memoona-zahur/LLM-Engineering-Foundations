2. **Income and Employment Stability**: Confirm the borrower's income, employment status, and job security to ensure they can afford the loan repayments.
3. **Debt-to-Income Ratio**: Calculate the borrower's debt-to-income ratio to ensure they have a manageable amount of debt and can afford the loan repayments without overextending themselves.
